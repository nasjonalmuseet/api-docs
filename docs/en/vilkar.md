# Terms of use

## Terms

You accept the terms when you create the key. The current version is on the
[registration page](https://api.nasjonalmuseet.no/api/v1/keys/register?lang=en) and at
<https://api.nasjonalmuseet.no/api/v1/terms>.

## What you can use the data for

Research, teaching, journalism, artistic work and development of services. You may store
and process the data locally.

## Credits

Name Nasjonalmuseet as the source where the data is shown, for example:

> Source: Nasjonalmuseet. Photo: Børre Høstland / Nasjonalmuseet.

The photographer is given in `multimedia[].photoCredit`.

## Images and copyright

That an image can be fetched through the API does not mean it is free to reuse. Copyright
in the works depicted follows its own rules. Check the rights for each work. Questions
about using images go to the museum.

## About the data

The catalogue data comes from a living collection catalogue with information gathered since the 1830s, and the API shows the records as they stand at the moment. Some records may be out of date. Some may contain language or ideas that today may be perceived as outdated, offensive or discriminatory, relating for example to gender, sexuality, ethnicity or disability, and that may be at odds with the museum's values of equality and diversity. The museum works continuously to improve the records and welcomes suggestions for improvement.

The numbers change with ongoing cataloguing. Extracting official figures and statistics about the collection from the API is therefore discouraged. If you need official figures, contact the museum.

AI-generated data at the ext level (keywords for semantic search) is machine-made and may contain errors.
